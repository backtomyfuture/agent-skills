## Style Prompt

Create a vertical mobile-first explainer for Bark as the last-mile notification layer for AI agents. The piece should feel precise, urgent, and trustworthy: dark graphite canvas, Apple-system notification language, sharp green delivery signals, and restrained data/UI motion. It should look like a premium technical product demo rather than a marketing landing page.

## Colors

- Background: `#06110E` deep graphite green
- Surface: `#0D211A` muted dark glass panel
- Foreground: `#F2FFE9` warm off-white
- Secondary text: `#A8B7AE` desaturated mint gray
- Primary accent: `#37F36B` Bark delivery green
- Alert accent: `#FFD166` time-sensitive amber
- Link/APNs accent: `#78A8FF` iOS blue

## Typography

- Chinese UI/headlines: `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, `Microsoft YaHei`
- Technical labels/code: `IBM Plex Mono`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`
- Headline weights should use 800-900; body copy should stay 300-500.

## Motion Rules

- Primary transition: fast green notification wipe, like a system alert sliding across the lock screen.
- Accent transition: short amber alert flash only for the mission-critical reveal.
- Scene entrances should feel like system UI assembling: slide, scale, count-up, and line-draw motions.
- Ambient motion should be subtle signal pulses and APNs route lines, not decorative gradients.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use purple/cyan neon gradients or generic AI-glow styling.
- Do not use stock phone screenshots unless real source assets are provided.
- Do not turn the comparison content into dense spreadsheet text.
- Do not use jump cuts between scenes.
